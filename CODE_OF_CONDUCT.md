# Code of conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with respect and technical clarity.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Give actionable feedback and accept it professionally.
- Protect private mailbox, credential, and organizational information.
- Focus disagreements on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, mailbox content, or security details.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer asks for behavior to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy.

For a confidential conduct report, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub Security Advisory and clearly label it as a code-of-conduct report. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.
